S1

Project Control & PMC

For developers, architects and interior firms

A structured execution-control layer between approved design documentation and site execution. Design stays with the architect; SSR makes what is built traceable to what was drawn.

  • BOQ prepared from the approved drawings, before work starts
  • BBS-controlled reinforcement with wastage tracked bar by bar
  • MEP sleeve pre-mapping before casting, so nothing is core-cut later
  • IS:456 pour cards signed off before every pour
  • GPS-tagged daily progress report dispatched at 19:00 every site day
  • Weekly progress report: planned-vs-actual, earned value (SPI / CPI / EAC), RFI log, NCR register, material reconciliation, risk register, photo log

Fees are a percentage of project cost or a fixed retainer — never a margin on materials. Send drawings, get a BOQ back: that is usually the first step.

S2

RCC Execution

For homeowners, developers and main contractors

Footings to roof slab, executed to the drawing and recorded as it happens.

  • Excavation and PCC blinding; isolated and combined footings; column starters cast with the footing where the design allows
  • Plinth beams with waterproofing, plinth slab and backfilling
  • Columns, beams, slabs and staircases, floor cycle by floor cycle
  • Reinforcement cut and bent to a bar-bending schedule; cube testing on every pour
  • Shuttering plans issued per slab, including sunken areas and voids
  • Brickwork, plaster and follow-on trades coordinated against the same programme

Every stage is photographed and appears in that day's report, so the owner sees the slab before it is covered.

S3

Firefighting & Detection

For societies, developers and commercial buildings

Wet risers, sprinklers and detection, planned with the structure rather than cut into it afterwards.

  • IS:1239 wet risers and hydrant lines
  • Sprinkler networks with zone control and flow switches
  • Fire detection and alarm systems
  • Sleeve pre-embedment during the RCC stage — no core cutting later
  • Testing, commissioning and handover documentation

Where SSR also runs the RCC, the two programmes are coordinated from one drawing set.
