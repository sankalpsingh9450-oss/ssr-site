How a job runs

One chain, from drawing to site

Design stays with your architect. SSR sits between the approved drawings and the site, and keeps a record at every step.

  1. 01Approved drawings

    We build from the issued set and log every revision.

  2. 02BOQ

    Quantities measured from the drawings before work is priced.

  3. 03BBS

    A bar bending schedule for every footing, column, beam and slab.

  4. 04Material control

    Steel and concrete reconciled against the BBS and BOQ.

  5. 05Site execution

    An IS 456 pour card, signed by the proprietor, before every pour.

  6. 06Measurement

    Work measured on site against the BOQ, not estimated.

  7. 07Daily progress report

    Photos, manpower and progress, sent every site day.

  8. 08RFI & NCR

    Questions and non-conformances logged until they are closed.

  9. 09Weekly report

    Planned against actual, with photographs and a look-ahead.

  10. 10Cost & schedule

    Earned value tracked, so slippage shows early.

The same chain runs on a G+3 house and on a tower. On a small job the reports are shorter; the steps stay.
