How a model is built for your project

  1. Step 1

    You share drawings

    DWG or PDF plans, elevations and sections. A brochure set is enough for a first pass.

  2. Step 2

    SSR models to the drawings

    The building, its floors and interiors, modelled as drawn. Where an assumption is made, it is labelled inside the model.

  3. Step 3

    You receive a link or a file

    A shareable link, or a single .html file that opens offline on a laptop, tablet or phone. Design stays entirely with you.

Typical uses
  • Client presentations and approvals
  • Developer sales before a show flat exists
  • A shared model the site team can read

Opening a model

Open the link in Chrome, Edge or Safari on a laptop or phone — nothing to install.

Drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom, two-finger drag to pan. The buttons at the bottom of each model switch between section, plan, flats, night and tour views.

Prefer a file? The same models can be supplied as single .html files that open offline.
