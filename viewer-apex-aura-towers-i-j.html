Each tower is a pinwheel of four wings around a 34' × 34' core. Every wing holds a mirrored pair of flats with a shared lift-lobby landing; a light-well runs between the pair. Tower J carries the large 3 BHK plans, Tower I the compact 2/3 BHK plans — exactly as coloured on the brochure site plan.
